Drumming

I have been drumming since I was about 4 years old. It is one of the things I enjoy most. To keep the family happy, I no longer play on an acoustic set - I now have a Roland TD-17 KVX kit which I use to practice and record.

The following tracks are ones I've particularly enjoyed playing along to. I have taken "drumless" tracks from elsewhere and have added the drums to them.

All the drums in the following tracks are just me, without editing. As such, sometimes the drum levels are a bit too loud.

I'll update this periodically as I record new covers.
